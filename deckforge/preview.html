Android home screen and launch screen

Android crops the maskable icon to whatever shape the phone uses. The last icon shows what happens when only a standard icon exists. No maskable icons were generated, so Android shrinks the standard icon onto a white plate. The launch screen is built by Android from background_color, the 512px icon and the app name.

Everything important must sit inside the red dashed circle. Empty space between the artwork and that circle means the icon will look small on the phone.

iPhone and iPad
